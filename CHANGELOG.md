# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — 2026-09-29

The first complete edition.

- Front Page: the masthead roll, day gauge and evening edition; sections I/II with folios; ink strike by tap or swipe; margin words; hold-to-reorder; a 5-second undo; the colophon with a standfirst.
- Composer: plain-English quick add with chips (dates, times, `every …`, `#tags`, `by …`, `tonight`). Tap a chip to discard it.
- Entry sheet with sub-entries, deadline, repeat, reminder and tags; When sheet; Quick Find (Ctrl/⌘+K); a full keyboard map.
- Week Spread and Someday; the Ledger with streaks, strength rings, a heatmap, weekly habits and freeze tokens; Back Issues with frozen issues.
- Colophon settings: Night/Proof/Auto, evening hour, rollover, motion, synthesised sound, reminders, backups.
- Backups: JSON export/import, Markdown export, GitHub Gist backup and restore with merge, 6-hourly auto-backup; a share card PNG.
- Android: Capacitor 8 project, reminders with Done/Snooze, the daily review, deep links, icons and splash; APK built and signed in CI.
